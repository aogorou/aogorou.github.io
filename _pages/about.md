I am **Aoi Murakami**, a Specially Appointed Researcher in the Department of Earth and Space Science, Graduate School of Science, **The University of Osaka**.

I study how photosynthetic organisms shape planetary environments, and how their light signatures could help us detect life beyond Earth. My work connects **plant physiology, remote sensing, and planetary science**, from energy flow within a leaf to signals observed across a lake or a planet.

## Research interests

- **Photosynthetic energy:** heat dissipation through non-photochemical quenching (NPQ) and its physiological and environmental effects.
- **Remote biosignatures:** the vegetation red edge of floating aquatic plants, studied with spectroscopy, drones, and satellite observations.
- **Planetary coevolution:** the links between the spread of photosynthetic life and changes in planetary environments, explored through numerical modeling.
