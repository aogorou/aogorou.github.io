My research asks how photosynthetic life changes its environment and how those changes can be observed. I connect measurements of living organisms with observations and models of planetary environments.

## Photosynthetic energy and heat dissipation

Non-photochemical quenching (NPQ) dissipates excess light energy as heat. I study the amount of heat produced and its implications for plant physiology and environmental energy budgets.

Our 2024 study used simplified models to examine NPQ-related heat production across leaf and planetary scales.

[Read the paper](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpls.2024.1367795)

## Floating vegetation as a biosignature

Can we detect photosynthetic life on a planet with little exposed land? I investigate floating aquatic vegetation as a potential surface biosignature, using the vegetation red edge and its seasonal variability.

This work combines leaf-level spectroscopy, field and drone observations, and satellite time series to connect biological properties with remotely observable signals.

[Read the paper](https://doi.org/10.1089/ast.2024.0127)

## Photosynthesis and planetary coevolution

I am interested in how the expansion of photosynthetic organisms changes planetary environments over time. Numerical models help connect biological processes, biogeochemical cycles, and atmospheric chemistry, with implications for the interpretation of biosignatures.

[Doctoral thesis](https://ir.soken.ac.jp/records/2000328) · [GitHub profile](https://github.com/aogorou)
